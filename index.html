About the project

You are in Berlin. Riding the U8.

You hold a phone. The battery is draining. Around you, zombie-like figures stare — the Smombies. They approach when you look at them. They retreat when you look at your phone.

Keep scrolling. Stay safe. Arrive at your destination with a full battery — and discover what you've become.

"Smombie" — a portmanteau of "Smartphone" and "Zombie" — was voted German Youth Word of the Year in 2015. It describes people so absorbed in their phones they become oblivious to the world around them.
